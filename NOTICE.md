# Third-party provenance and licensing scope

The MIT license in this repository covers original software and documentation
created by the `vdw-search` contributors. It does not supersede any rights in
material transcribed from or attributed to third-party publications.

## Ahmed–Kullmann–Snevily certificate sources

Files matching `sources/aks_v4_t*.compact` are machine-readable transcriptions
of certificate strings in the version 4 arXiv source appendix of:

> M. Ahmed, O. Kullmann, and H. Snevily, “On the van der Waerden numbers
> w(2;3,t),” *Discrete Applied Mathematics* 174 (2014), 27–51.
> <https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.5433>

Files matching `certs/aks_w2_3_*.txt` are mechanical expansions of those
strings into this repository's verifier format. They are included for
attribution, verification, comparison, and reproducibility.

## Komkov certificate sources

Files matching `certs/komkov_*.txt` are verifier-format transcriptions of
certificates published in:

> A. Komkov, “New Lower Bounds for Van der Waerden Numbers,”
> <https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.05603>

They are included for attribution, verification, comparison, and
reproducibility.

All other certificate provenance is recorded in the comment header of the
certificate itself and in `records.md`.
