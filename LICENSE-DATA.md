contributors have waived all copyright and related or neighboring rights in
the original machine-readable research data identified below by dedicating it
to the public domain under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public

The dedication covers:

- original certificate and integrity-manifest data in `certs/`;
- original seed and model data in `seeds/`; and
- original generated certificates, candidate states, SAT models, CNF files,

Source code and scripts found in those directories remain covered by the MIT
license in `LICENSE`.

## Third-party exclusions

The CC0 dedication does **not** apply to material transcribed from or
attributed to third-party publications, including:

- `sources/aks_v4_t*.compact`;
- `certs/aks_w2_3_*.txt`; and
- `certs/komkov_*.txt`.

Those files are included for attribution, verification, comparison, and
reproducibility. Their provenance and scope are described in `NOTICE.md`.
Where another data file identifies third-party provenance in its header, that
notice also takes precedence over this dedication.
